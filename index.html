Shape Tower

Your core sits in the middle. Shapes drift in from every side. Your core fires automatically, aiming at your pointer.

Each shape's HP doubles as you go up the list. Kills earn points. Buy worlds to multiply your points, and buy turrets and quake shockwaves to help. Buy every world to unlock Rebirth, a permanent boost.

Turrets only fire when you control them: click one (or press its number) to take control, then hold to fire.

Press U to open upgrades and settings. The game pauses while you shop.
